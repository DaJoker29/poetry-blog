It feels rare that good things happen to me
Without me having to pursue or manuever for them.
#Blessed but none of it feels like it was fortune or luck.

That's why it felt so special when she asked the leading question, "Are you single?"
It felt like Hera saw the wounds and threw me a bandage,
In a form of dark hair, starry-eyes and a mischievous smile.

I scan through a million fantasies in a million worlds to fall asleep at night,
And in none of them had I dreamt of her wide-eyed gaze
As she nervously obsessed over which Unicorn to play next.
Nor could I have dreamt that would be the moment my eyes opened.

My feelings are now in a basket in front of me; can't answer what is what.
All I know is when I say I her name, I feel hot.
Not in the sexy way. But like middle of summer:
muggy, humid, mosquitoes coming around for seconds.
An uncomfortable tension follows me, I can't escape it...
And I don't want to.

Because when waking from a decades-long dream,
Imperfect reality is better than falling back to sleep.
