I think deep down every dragon wants to be slain.
Defeated, at least. They want to lay eyes on their
Conquerer, and know it took someone formidible
To vanquish them. And, in that,
They know that they were great.

Deep down every dragon wants their name forever claimed
By someone mighty. That's why some are slain in battle,
And others develop beer bellies and their own imprints on the sofa.
