Numbers were his first love, Words came later.

People often ran from Numbers; not appreciating their complexity.
But he loved their nuance: adding, subtracting, dividing, multiplying;
He’d watch them shift into familiar and comforting patterns.

Workbooks grew repetitive but he found Numbers everywhere:
Street addresses, license plates, menu prices, and more.
He’d watch them dance and rearrange themselves to fall asleep at night.

Then one day Words arrived and became his new obsession…Why?
Because Numbers came easy, but Words required coaxing.
Finding the right Words demanded more from him.

He wrote stories, poems, philosophy, and rhetoric; and each one challenged him.
Each one continuously demanded a wiser, kinder, greater version of himself.
He loved the challenge Words provided and arose each day with a smile to meet it.

Numbers were his first love, but Words stole his heart.
Until one day She arrived and filled the space between them both.
