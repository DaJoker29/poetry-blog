Once upon a time,
A Phoenix saw a throng gathered in a swamp;
Curious, the Phoenix flew down to investigate.
She discovered men, women, and children; all standing in line
To see and converse with The Man in the Rounded Glass Box.

The Box was a strange attraction, so translucent and round;
The Man inside even more strange, for he refused to ever leave it.
A kind, helpful man: he bore the wisdom of a well-traveled soul,
Yet he never spoke of what lives he'd led before. He would chat and give counsel,
But all that truly mattered to him was that he stayed inside the Glass Box,
For its what kept him safe, he claimed.

This fascinated the Phoenix; how oddly peculiar, she thought.
She asked him to join her for an adventure or two.
Hesitant, though not lacking in bravery, The Man agreed.
The two traveled far and wide together on many adventures;
The Man bringing his sacred Rounded Glass Box
Everywhere they went.

Eventually, the man grew to trust the Phoenix, relied on her for his safety,
And thus felt safe enough to remove the Rounded Glass Box...

The Phoenix was overjoyed at seeing The Man shed his protective layer,
So overjoyed that she burned bright with fiery delight.
But the blaze grew too strong and The Man was badly burned by the Phoenix's flame.
The Phoenix watched in terror as The Man quickly retreated back into his Rounded Glass Box.

Days later, the Phoenix went to check on The Man's recovery.
Along the way, her heart sank—
In the distance, a young mighty dragon soared away from the swamp.
The Phoenix raced to the swamp and was heartbroken at what she found.

Destroyed, utterly.
Every surface scorched. The Rounded Glass Box,
Empty and shattered to pieces.
Zero traces of her dear friend.

Nothing could calm her, she felt immensely alone,
Until she looked down and realized...
The Rounded Glass Box had held a secret this whole time,
She realized, it wasn't made of glass,

It'd been an egg all along.
