title: "Sorry Friend"
date: 2026-08-20T10:45:41-07:00
draft: false
---

You wanted friendship; a warm glow
To guide you to through the night.
I wanted love; crazed craving for
Someone I could hold tight.

I didn't listen. Deafened
by daydreams of domesticity.

A source of comfort
Now brought more pain.
I didn't see what I became
Until your door was closed tight.

Now I'm the one searching
For the light.
