Sorry Friend

August 20, 2026

You wanted friendship; a warm glow To guide you to through the night. I wanted love; crazed craving for Someone I could hold tight.

I didn’t listen. Deafened by daydreams of domesticity.

A source of comfort Now brought more pain. I didn’t see what I became Until your door was closed tight.

Now I’m the one searching For the light.

D. Buckingham
